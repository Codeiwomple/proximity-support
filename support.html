Common questions

How do I steer?

Play with your device in landscape. During a flight, hold the left half of the screen to move left or the right half to move right. You do not need to hit the small arrows. Release to stop steering; holding both sides cancels the steering input.

Why is there no sound?

Check that the game shows “Sound on”, raise the media volume and check where audio is being played in Control Centre. Try with Silent Mode off and with headphones disconnected. If it is still silent, please report your device model, iOS version and whether the problem happens immediately or after switching apps.

What happens when I switch apps?

The flight pauses when the app is interrupted. Return to the game and choose Resume when you are ready. If the app closes, the interrupted ranked flight’s score can be recovered; the flight itself starts again.

Are my records shared between devices?

No. Records and your sound preference are saved locally. There is no online account or cloud leaderboard. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting, because that removes local records.

How do I delete my saved data?

Deleting the app removes its local records and settings. Offloading it keeps its data. Any device backups are managed separately through your Apple backup settings.

Contact support

Ask a question or report a problem on the Proximity support tracker.

Contact support on GitHub

You’ll need a GitHub account to post. Reports and replies are public, so please share only technical details. A parent or guardian should submit support requests for children.

For a bug report, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The Proximity version you are using.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Whether it happens every time, and whether you had just switched apps or audio devices.

Please leave out passwords, payment details and personal information about children. See the privacy policy for how support information is handled.
