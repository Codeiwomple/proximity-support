Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Proximity is an offline game. No account is needed, and your flight records stay on your device.

While you play

Proximity saves your best flight time, local leaderboard, an interrupted flight’s score and your sound preference on your device. The game does not send these records to the developer or provide an online leaderboard.

The game has no advertising, tracking or analytics service enabled. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. It does not collect names or email addresses during play.

Keeping and deleting game records

Local records remain until the app’s data is removed. On iPhone or iPad, deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app keeps it. Device backups may retain app data according to your Apple backup settings. Proximity has no developer-operated cloud save service.

If you contact support

Support uses GitHub Issues. Posting requires a GitHub account. Your GitHub username, report and replies are public. The developer uses the information you choose to post to answer your request and investigate problems.

Please share only technical details such as your device model, app version and steps to reproduce a problem. Do not post email addresses, passwords, payment information or personal information about children. A parent or guardian should submit support requests for children.

Reports remain on GitHub until removed. You can edit your comments, request removal through the support tracker, and use GitHub’s account and privacy controls. GitHub’s own retention policies apply to data it holds; removing a public post cannot remove copies made by others.

Apple services

App Store downloads, purchases, device backups and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled under Apple’s privacy information and your device settings.

This website

These support pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or embedded third-party content. They do not set cookies or use browser storage.

On GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of website traffic and support-account information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links takes you to services with their own privacy policies.

Children

The game’s local-only data handling applies to all players. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. A parent or guardian can contact support about any information they believe was shared through a support request.

Changes and questions

This page will be updated if the game’s data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. For questions about this policy, contact the Proximity developer.
