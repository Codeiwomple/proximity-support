Mountain terrain

Mapzen Terrain Tiles. Produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union — EU-DEM layers.

The elevation data has been rotated, scaled, resampled and adapted for game scenery, including changes to the foreground and surface shading. It is not a navigation or mapping product. No endorsement by the European Union or the data providers is implied.

Surface materials

Snow 02, Forrest Ground 01, Forest Floor, Rock Face 03, Bark Brown 02, Fir Tree 01 and Fern 02 from Poly Haven, available under CC0 1.0 Universal. Texture maps have been adapted for the game; the original tree and fern models are not used.

Alpine pines

Pine_1, Pine_2 and Pine_5 from Stylized Nature MegaKit — Standard by Quaternius, under CC0 1.0 Universal. Models have been adapted with revised scale, foliage, colours and snow cover.
